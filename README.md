## 📈 Key Results
Running the model on NVDA with default assumptions (9% WACC, 6% FCF 
growth, 2.5% terminal growth) implies a fair value of $74.31 vs a 
market price of $230.36 — a 67.7% downside. This large gap 
highlights a key limitation of standard DCF: a flat 6% growth 
assumption significantly understates NVIDIA's recent growth 
trajectory (recent revenue growth has exceeded 100% YoY), showing 
how sensitive DCF outputs are to growth inputs, especially for 
high-growth companies where the market is pricing in future 
performance far above historical averages.
